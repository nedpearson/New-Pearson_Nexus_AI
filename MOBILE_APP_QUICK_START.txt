📸 PHOTO CAPTURE:
   1. Tap "Take Photo" (cyan button)
   2. Camera opens instantly
   3. Capture evidence
   4. Tap "Save Evidence"
   ✓ Done!

🎤 VOICE-TO-NOTES:
   1. Tap "Voice Note" (purple button)
   2. Recording starts automatically
   3. Speak clearly
   4. Use Pause/Resume as needed
   5. Tap "Stop & Save"
   6. Review auto-transcription
   7. Tap "Save Evidence"
   ✓ Transcribed & Saved!

📤 FILE UPLOAD:
   1. Tap "Upload File" (green button)
   2. Select from phone
   3. Tap "Save Evidence"
   ✓ Uploaded!
