## Where Pearson Nexus AI can win (clear differentiators)
A) **Proof-first outputs**: Every recommendation, eligibility claim, or plan must show sources + linked documents. (Most tools summarize; fewer provide auditable proof.)
B) **Funding + credits as an operational pipeline**: not just “you might qualify,” but:
   - why you qualify
   - what evidence is needed
   - prefilled packet
   - submission links + tracking
C) **Simple View / Advanced View**: keep onboarding non-scary while still supporting power users.
D) **Weekly policy updates with review queue**: maintain freshness safely via curated sources and admin approval.
E) **One life OS**: combine documents + bills + tasks + calendar + legal cases + funding.

## “What customers complain about elsewhere” (opportunity themes)
- Tools that feel too complex for non-technical users
- Advice without citations/sources
- Too many disconnected tools (finance separate from documents, legal separate from planning)
- Grant/credit info is hard to act on (no checklist, no packet, no workflow)

## Go-to-market (practical, low-cost)
- Launch with a narrow promise: “Never miss money you qualify for — with proof and one-click packets.”
- Lead magnets: free templates + eligibility checklists + “proof-first” demo workspace.
- Channels:
  - SEO content: credits/grants/loans + state/entity questions
  - Partnerships: accountants/bookkeepers, small business communities
  - Product-led growth: demo mode + shareable reports/packets
