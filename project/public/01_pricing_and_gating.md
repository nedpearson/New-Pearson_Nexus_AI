# Pricing structure + gating (recommended)

## Tiers
- Personal Plus
- Business Starter
- Business Sync
- Business Growth
- Business Elite
- Enterprise

## Add-ons
- QuickBooks Sync
- Funding Navigator
- Prefilled Packets
- Advisory Workflows

## Recommended gating decisions
- Templates Basic is NOT included in Personal Plus.
- Citations are REQUIRED starting at Business Growth.
- Elite adds stricter review workflows + audit trail.

## Why
- Keeps the beginner experience simple.
- Creates clean upgrade paths (Sync -> Growth -> Elite).
