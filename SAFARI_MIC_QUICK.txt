═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  HOW TO ENABLE MICROPHONE ON iPHONE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

METHOD 1: In Safari (Quick):
  1. Tap [aA] icon in address bar
  2. Tap "Website Settings"
  3. Find "Microphone" → Select "Allow"
  4. Reload page

METHOD 2: iOS Settings:
  1. iPhone Settings → Safari
  2. "Camera & Microphone Access"
  3. Microphone → "Ask" or "Allow"
  4. Return to app and reload
