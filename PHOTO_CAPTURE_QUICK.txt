  MULTIPLE PHOTO CAPTURE - QUICK REFERENCE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

📸 CAPTURE WORKFLOW:

  1. Tap "Take Photo" or "Upload File"
     → Select/capture first photo

  2. Tap "Add More" to capture additional photos
     → Repeat as many times as needed

  3. Add Notes (REQUIRED):
     ✓ Type text notes, OR
     ✓ Record voice notes, OR
     ✓ Both!

  4. Review photo grid
     → Tap X on any photo to remove

  5. Tap "Save X Photos"
     → All saved together offline

  6. When ready: Tap "Sync Now"
     → All photos + notes upload as one item

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  KEY FEATURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  ✅ Multiple photos at once
  ✅ Add more photos anytime
  ✅ Remove individual photos
  ✅ Text OR voice notes required
  ✅ Photo grid with thumbnails
  ✅ Character counter for notes
  ✅ Save button disabled without notes
  ✅ All photos sync together

  VALIDATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  ✅ CAN SAVE:
     • Photos + text notes
     • Photos + voice notes
     • Photos + text + voice
     • Voice notes alone
